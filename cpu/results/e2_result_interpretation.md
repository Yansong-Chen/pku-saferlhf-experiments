# E2 result interpretation

The primary analysis conditions on the 76,125 response positions released as
unsafe. It retains seven factors by the 1,000-simulation parallel-analysis
rule; the one-response-per-pair unsafe sensitivity analysis also retains
seven. The loading patterns are closely similar across the two matrices.

The raw tetrachoric matrix has a minimum eigenvalue of -3.2686 and therefore
requires smoothing before factor extraction. Two retained factors are dominated
by a single category: Cybercrime and Endangering Public Health each have an
absolute loading of approximately one. These facts make a seven-factor latent
taxonomy claim unsuitable.

The EFA is retained as a reproducibility and sparsity diagnostic. The primary
E2 evidence in the dissertation should instead be the conditional
co-occurrence matrix, solo-category rates, and category-specific severity
profiles. Those outputs directly describe the moderation-facing release
without treating a smoothed sparse correlation matrix as a natural structure
of harm.
